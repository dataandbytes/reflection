How it works

A few quiet minutes a day, alone with yourself.

One honest question a day, and then the app leaves you alone. You don't answer here. You write it down, by hand, in a notebook. There's no account and no feed, and nothing you write is ever stored.

01

One question a day

Each day there's one question. Just one, and it's picked so you can't shrug it off in a sentence. You won't see the same one again until you've been through them all. Then it starts over.

Open the app, read it, and let it sit. That's the whole screen. Nothing to tap through, nothing to scroll.

02

Answer on paper, not here

This is the part that matters. Pick up a pen and write your answer out by hand. Writing is slow, and slow is the point. You catch things longhand that you'd skate right past on a keyboard.

What you write never goes into the app. It stays in your notebook, which is the only place it belongs: private, and yours.

03

Choose your time

Tap "Set a daily time" on the main screen and pick when you want to do this. First coffee, the train, the last thing before you sleep. Whatever you'll actually keep.

It builds a repeating reminder you drop into your phone's own calendar. The reminder lives there, not in the app, so it still goes off when the app is closed.

Add this page to your home screen and it opens like an app. On iPhone, use Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the menu and tap Add to Home screen.
04

The categories

The questions wander through the parts of a life worth slowing down for. Over time you'll get asked from each of these:

Identity & Self Relationships & Love Fear & Vulnerability Growth & Change Work & Purpose The Past Mortality & Meaning Joy & Gratitude Values & Integrity Desire & Longing Solitude & Stillness The Body & Being
05

Nothing is saved

No sign-up, no server. The one thing your device keeps is a short list of which questions you've seen, so you don't get the same one twice. Not your answers. Nothing about you.

Clear your browser data and even that list is gone. The only record this leaves is the one you write in ink.

06

Make it your own

Every question sits in one file, js/questions.js, as a plain list of { category, text } entries. Add your own, delete the ones that don't land, rewrite whatever you want. The app just reads the list.

Keep them open-ended. If a question can be answered in a word, it's too easy. A good one makes you explain yourself.
