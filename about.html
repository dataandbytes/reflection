How it works

A few quiet minutes, every day, with only yourself.

Reflection asks you one honest question a day — and then gets out of the way. You don't type your answer here. You write it, by hand, in a notebook. No account, no feed, nothing to scroll, and nothing of yours ever stored.

01

One question a day

Each day you're given a single question, drawn to resist a one-line answer. You won't see the same one twice until you've worked through every question — then it quietly begins again.

Open the app, read it once, and let it sit with you. That's the whole screen. There's deliberately nothing else to do here.

02

Answer on paper, not here

This is the heart of it. Take a pen and a notebook and write your answer by hand. Writing slowly makes you think slowly — you notice things you'd skip past while typing.

Nothing you write is ever entered into the app. Your reflections live only in your notebook, where they belong — private, yours, and un-searchable.

03

Choose your time

Tap “Set a daily time” on the main screen and pick the moment you want to meet yourself each day — first coffee, the commute, last thing at night.

It hands you a recurring reminder you add to your phone's own calendar. The reminder lives in your calendar, not in the app, so it fires reliably even when the app is closed.

Tip: add this page to your home screen so it opens like an app. On iPhone — Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android — menu → Add to Home screen.
04

The categories

The questions move across the parts of a life that are worth sitting with. Over time you'll be asked from each of these:

Identity & Self Relationships & Love Fear & Vulnerability Growth & Change Work & Purpose The Past Mortality & Meaning Joy & Gratitude Values & Integrity Desire & Longing Solitude & Stillness The Body & Being
05

Nothing is saved

There's no sign-up and no server. The only thing kept on your device is a short list of which questions you've already seen, so you don't repeat — never your answers, never anything about you.

Clear your browser data and even that list resets. The practice leaves no trace but the one you make in ink.

06

Make it your own

Every question lives in a single file, js/questions.js, as a short list of { category, text } entries. Add your own, cut the ones that don't land, reword freely — the app simply uses whatever's in the list.

Keep them open-ended. A good question can't be answered in a word; it asks you to describe, trace, or reckon with something.
